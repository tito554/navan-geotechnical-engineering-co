Geotechnical laboratory testing in Navan provides essential soil classification data to support safe foundation design across the region’s variable glacial tills and limestone-derived subsoils. Our facility carries out physical property tests compliant with IS EN ISO 17892 standards, beginning with precise grain size analysis (sieve + hydrometer) to define particle distribution from coarse gravels down to silt and clay fractions. This is routinely paired with Atterberg limits to determine liquid and plastic limits, directly quantifying the cohesive soil behaviour and shrink-swell potential prevalent in local lodgement tills.

These index tests form the critical baseline for residential, commercial, and infrastructure projects throughout County Meath, from single-house builds on boulder clay to road widening schemes requiring robust CBR and compaction verification. The resulting parameters feed directly into bearing capacity calculations and settlement assessments, ensuring compliance with Technical Guidance Document A and the National Annex to Eurocode 7. For a complete geotechnical model, routine grain size analysis is often integrated with chemical testing and strength profiling to de-risk ground conditions before construction commences.
